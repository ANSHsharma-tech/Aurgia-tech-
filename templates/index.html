Pricing Engine Financial Rules & GST Spec

1. Seating Tiers & Capacity Lockout

Seats are categorized into Silver, Gold, and Recliner. When a tier sells out (remaining capacity = 0), the counter system locks booking for that tier to eliminate overbooking and queue disputes.

2. Discount Layering & Caps
  • Festival Offer: Flat ₹50.00 off base ticket total.
  • Member Offer: 15% discount on base tickets, capped at maximum ₹100.00 deduction.
  • Rule: Total discounts cannot exceed gross ticket price (tickets can never be negative).
3. Convenience Fee

A fixed fee of ₹30.00 per ticket is added to every booking to cover counter booking infrastructure and operations.

4. Indian GST Dual-Slab Splitting (Exact Paisa)
  • Tickets: 18% GST (9% CGST + 9% SGST) applied to net ticket price after all discounts.
  • Convenience Fee: 18% Service GST (9% CGST + 9% SGST) applied to total convenience fee.
  • Exact Paisa Arithmetic: Decimal math with half-up rounding guarantees every paisa balances to the penny with zero floating point drift.
